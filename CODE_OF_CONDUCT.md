# Code of conduct

Magnife follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** People here come from different stacks, languages and levels of experience. Questions that seem basic are welcome.
- **Critique code, not people.** Explain the why, and suggest a better way.
- **No harassment of any kind.** That includes insults, personal or political attacks, unwelcome sexual attention, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately on [GitHub](https://github.com/NsRF) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nasserfarhat/). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeat or seriously break it, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
